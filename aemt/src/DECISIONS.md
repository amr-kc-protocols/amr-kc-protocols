# Source decisions for chapters not yet written

Settled questions that were blocking content. Recorded here so the chapter that
uses them is built on the decision rather than on whatever was to hand.

## Pediatric vital sign ranges — Chapter 9.6

**Decided: the 2025 AHA/AAP guidelines and the PALS materials derived from them.**

- *Part 8: Pediatric Advanced Life Support: 2025 American Heart Association and
  American Academy of Pediatrics Guidelines for Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation
  and Emergency Cardiovascular Care.* Circulation, October 2025.
- *Part 6: Pediatric Basic Life Support*, same guideline set (Pediatrics, 2025).

Released 22 October 2025, co-developed with the AAP — the first full update
since 2020, with the PALS course materials revised alongside it.

### What is verified, and what still has to be transcribed

**Hypotension thresholds — unchanged in the 2025 update, and safe to author:**

| Age | Systolic hypotension |
| --- | --- |
| Term neonate, 0–28 days | < 60 mmHg |
| Infant, 1–12 months | < 70 mmHg |
| Child, 1–10 years | < 70 + (2 × age in years) mmHg |
| Over 10 years | < 90 mmHg |

**New in 2025, and worth knowing as context even though it is outside AEMT
scope** (both require invasive arterial monitoring):

- Diastolic pressure targets during CPR — ≥ 25 mmHg in infants, ≥ 30 mmHg in
  older children — the first time an objective perfusion target appears in the
  pediatric guidelines.
- Post-arrest: maintain systolic and mean arterial pressure above the 10th
  percentile for age.

**Not yet verified — do not author from memory or from a prep site.** The
per-age *normal* heart rate and respiratory rate tables (awake and asleep bands
by age group) must be transcribed from the PALS 2025 provider manual or the AHA
reference material itself. The 2025 update concentrated on resuscitation
practice — shock dosing, airway, rhythm, ECPR, post-ROSC care — rather than on
redefining normal ranges, but "probably unchanged" is not a source. Transcribe
the tables, then write 9.6.

Until that is done, chapter 9 stays on seed content and the block says so.
