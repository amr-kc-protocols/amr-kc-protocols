How the evidence is graded

Claims in this course carry an evidence-strength badge, so you can see how firm the ground is. Weak evidence is labelled as weak — and occasionally still supports a strong recommendation, which is said explicitly where it applies.

StrongLarge or consistent randomized evidence, or an aligned guideline position.
ModerateReasonable evidence with real limitations — single trials, or consistent observational data.
LimitedCase reports, mechanism, or consensus only. Treat conclusions cautiously.
ContestedGood-quality studies that disagree. Reasonable clinicians differ.
