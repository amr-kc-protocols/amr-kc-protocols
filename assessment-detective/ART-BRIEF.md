# Assessment Detective — scene art brief

One image per case, shown on the first-impression screen. The learner makes
their first decisions from it, so the clinical cues listed under each scene
have to be visible, and nothing in the picture should give the diagnosis
away.

## Specs (match the first three)

- **Size:** portrait, 1024 × 1536 (2:3). Photo-realistic, natural light,
  lived-in home or public setting.
- **Framing:** on a phone the game shows a 4:3 crop of the upper-middle of
  the image (roughly 12 % to 62 % of the height). Keep faces and every
  must-show cue inside that band.
- **People:** the patient and one bystander. No EMS crew, no equipment
  that hasn't arrived yet.
- **No** readable text, logos, brand names or labels (generic bottles only).
- **Delivery:** send the PNG; it gets converted to `.webp` under 250 KB and
  saved as the file name below.

## 4 · Throat Feels Tight — `throat-tight-scene.webp`

**Setting:** a busy, casual restaurant in the evening. Warm light, other
diners blurred in the background. A half-eaten plate of shrimp noodles on
the table.

**Patient:** a man in his mid-30s, seated at the table, leaning slightly
forward.

**Must show:**
- one hand at his throat
- flushed face; raised red welts (hives) on his neck and forearms
- slightly puffy lips and eyelids
- frightened, wide-eyed expression, mouth open as if struggling to speak

**Bystander:** a friend across the table, half standing, pushing the plate
away, worried.

**Avoid:** anyone performing the Heimlich, an EpiPen in view, or vomit.

## 5 · Just Heartburn — `heartburn-scene.webp`

**Setting:** an open suburban garage on a hot, bright afternoon. A push
mower just outside on cut grass.

**Patient:** a man in his late 50s, heavy-set, sitting on the garage step
in a sweat-soaked T-shirt and work clothes.

**Must show:**
- a fist pressed under his breastbone / upper belly
- pale, gray skin with heavy sweat on his face (clearly more than heat)
- an expression that is uncomfortable but dismissive, waving his wife off
  with the other hand

**Bystander:** his wife standing over him, holding a generic white antacid
bottle, worried.

**Avoid:** clutching the left arm or chest (too obvious), cigarettes in view.

## 6 · Just Weak — `just-weak-scene.webp`

**Setting:** a small, tidy living room in late morning, curtains half
drawn. A side table with a pill organizer and a full, untouched glass of
water.

**Patient:** a frail woman in her early 80s, slumped in a recliner under a
knitted blanket.

**Must show:**
- eyes half open, drowsy, head tilted, not engaging
- flushed cheeks; looks feverish rather than pale
- one bare foot visible below the blanket, with mottled, blotchy skin

**Bystander:** a home caregiver in scrubs kneeling beside the chair,
holding her hand and a phone.

**Avoid:** blood, vomit, a visible urine bag or catheter (it gives the
source away), facial droop.
