“Organ run” is not one call type.

It's three different missions — each with a different patient (or none), a different role for you, and a different way to chart it. Knowing which one you're on is the whole job. This short course gets you from “never heard of an organ run” to knowing the mission types, your role, who to call, and how to document it — before you're assigned one solo.

5 LESSONS KNOWLEDGE CHECK ~15 MIN

⚠Familiarization only. Clinical specifics — especially donor management — follow the current AMR KC Protocols (II-25 & Section VII), which govern. This module orients you to the mission types and your role; it does not replace the protocol or a sending physician's orders.
The one thing to get right
🧊 A A cooler, a perfusion device, and/or a recovery team — often no patient on board. → Recovery / logistics run. The clock (cold ischemia time) is everything.
🫁 B A ventilated, supported donor going to the recovery hospital / OR. → Donor patient transport. The goal is organ perfusion, not resuscitation of the person.
🏃 C A recipient who “got the call.” → Recipient transport. Usually stable — your job is not to delay.
