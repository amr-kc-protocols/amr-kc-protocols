Choose a level, or free-play the Test Lung
Level 1 — Tutorial
Beginner
Learn the controls step by step. Settings unlock progressively as you demonstrate each concept and answer a question.
›
Level 2 — Clinical Scenarios
Intermediate
7 real patient situations. All controls available. Read the clinical picture and correct the ventilator settings.
›
Level 3 — Advanced Management
Advanced
5 complex cases requiring multi-parameter decisions. Applied clinical judgment — no hand-holding.
›
Troubleshooting — DOPE
Failures
3 cases where something was working and stopped. Read the alarm, find the cause, fix what can be fixed at the vent — and recognize what cannot.
›
Shift — Mixed & Timed
Test
5 random cases from Levels 2, 3 and Troubleshooting, each with a new patient — size, lungs and starting volume change every shift. Graded like Level 3, on a clock.
›
Free Play — Test Lung
Sandbox
No missions. Dial any settings you want on adult or pediatric test lungs (healthy child, pediatric ARDS, status asthmaticus, infant bronchiolitis); the vent enforces only what it can physically deliver. Live plausibility readout, judged per kilogram for children.
